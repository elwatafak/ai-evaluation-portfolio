# Spanish (Mexico) Language Quality Evaluation

## Goal

Evaluate whether Spanish output is grammatically correct, clear, natural, and appropriate for a Mexican audience.

## Example task

**User prompt**

> Escribe un mensaje breve para avisarle a un cliente en México que su pedido ya está listo para recoger.

**Candidate response**

> Hola, tu pedido ya se encuentra preparado para ser retirado. Puedes pasar a por él cuando gustes. Gracias por tu compra.

## Evaluation

**Verdict:** Understandable, but localization can be improved.

### Observations

- The message is grammatically correct.
- "Pasar a por él" is understandable, but it is less typical in everyday Mexican Spanish than alternatives such as "pasar por él" or "venir a recogerlo."
- "Retirado" is valid Spanish, although "recoger" sounds more natural for this context in Mexico.
- The tone is polite and concise.

## Improved version

> Hola, tu pedido ya está listo. Puedes pasar a recogerlo cuando gustes. ¡Gracias por tu compra!

## Evaluation principles

For Mexican Spanish, I look at:

- grammar and punctuation;
- natural vocabulary;
- regional phrasing;
- clarity;
- tone appropriate to the context;
- unnecessary literal translations from English or other Spanish varieties.

Regional evaluation should focus on naturalness without treating every variation from Mexican usage as automatically incorrect.
